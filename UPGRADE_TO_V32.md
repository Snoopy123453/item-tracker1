# Product Hunter Pro v32

## Project Workflow & Approval Management

- Create and manage multiple projects.
- Track project number, client, manager, status, and target date.
- Build project-specific approval queues.
- Assign divisions/cost codes, reviewers, priorities, and due dates.
- Manage statuses from Needs review through Installed.
- Show completion, pending-review, and overdue metrics.
- Preserve status-change audit events with actor and timestamp.
- Export approval registers and audit logs to CSV.

No new secrets are required.
