# Product Hunter Pro v29 — Product Workspace

## Added
- Dedicated Product Workspace for reviewed products.
- Unified overview, source evidence, lifecycle timeline, notes, and status actions.
- Procurement lifecycle from Specified through Warranty.
- Product notes with authors and timestamps.
- Automatic timeline entries when evidence decisions and statuses are saved.
- Single-product JSON export.
- Knowledge Base storage for product events and notes.

## Deploy
Upload all files to the root of `item-tracker1`, replace older files, commit, and reboot Streamlit. No secret changes are required.
