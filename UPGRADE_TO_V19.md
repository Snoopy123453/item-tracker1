# Product Hunter Pro v19 — Professional Workspace

## Major changes

- Routes SearXNG/OmniSearch results into the retailer, technical-document, manufacturer, and local-supplier views.
- Adds a Microsoft/Adobe-inspired neutral workspace with a dark navigation rail, compact command bar, Fluent-style cards, tighter tables, and consistent spacing.
- Organizes results into tabs: Research overview, Offers, Documents & manufacturer, Suppliers, and Diagnostics.
- Renames result sections to describe what general web research can actually verify.
- Adds run-level routing counts and provider-health information.
- Keeps OmniSearch as the source of truth while preserving Excel, RFQ, purchase-tracking, spec-comparison, and project workflows.

## Upgrade

Upload all files in this folder to the root of the existing `item-tracker1` repository, replace the older files, commit, and reboot the Streamlit app. Existing Streamlit secrets remain unchanged.
